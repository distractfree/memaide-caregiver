### BridgeSocket test note
The plan's `BridgeSocketTest` used `runTest` (virtual clock) against real OkHttp threads — it
fast-forwards the timeout before the round trip completes. Rewritten to **real time**: plain
`@Test` + `CountDownLatch`, `onSubscription` to register the collector before `connect()` (the
inbound SharedFlow has no replay), and it closes the **server-side** WebSocket before
`server.shutdown()` (else MockWebServer hangs). Stable across reruns.
