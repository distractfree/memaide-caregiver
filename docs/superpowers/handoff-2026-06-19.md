### The one safety invariant to remember
The describer's flags are **advisory only**. They land in `VisionContext.advisory_flags`,
**never** in `flags`. `safety/escalation.py` drives deterministic escalation off `flags`
(critical flags, or any flag + silence). Merging advisory flags into `flags` would let a
hallucinated `person_on_floor` fire a 911 suggestion — so they stay separated by design.
The rule-based `flags` are still produced by `StubVisionCheck` (the real CV drop-in is
later).

The eval writes `docs/vision-eval-runs/run-<timestamp>/`:
- `comparison.md` — grouped by image: each frame once, then all 4 combos' label/description/
  flags/cost side by side. **This is the file to read to make the model/detail decision.**
- `<model>/<detail>/results.{json,md}` + a copy of every frame it used.
- `run.json` — per-combo total cost + mean latency.

A handful of frames × 4 combos is a few cents. `low` detail pins gpt-4o-mini at ~2,833
image tokens; `high` removes its cost advantage — hence the eval measures real cost.
