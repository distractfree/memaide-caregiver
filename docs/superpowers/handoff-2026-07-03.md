## Key learning that reshapes Part B
**Free-form `send_text` only delivers inside a 24h "customer service window"** (opened when the
recipient messages the business number). During a real emergency the caregiver won't have an open
window, so **the escalation alert MUST be a pre-approved template** (`send_template`), not free-form
text. `send_text` stays as the testing/within-window path. → Update the spec's Part B wiring to use
the template, and add the `send_template` variables gap (goal #1).
